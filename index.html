About me

I was born on a small island in the east where X-raymentalism was part of daily life. As my curiosity grew, I left to explore the world in search of secret knowledge. I spent years studying physics, mathematics, and philosophy, collecting theories at an alarming rate and skills of uncertain value. It wasn’t until I met a rather peculiar Hobbit that my true powers began to show. Now I’m here to share that secret knowledge and show what it takes to be an X-raymentalist.
