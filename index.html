About me

Driven and ambitious Japanese physicist with a multidisciplinary foundation in physics and materials science, specializing in Ultrafast X-ray Optics and their application to capture rapid processes in areas such as fusion research, aerospace engineering, manufacturing, and medical imaging. Achievements include winning the Golden Proton Award in an International Science Competition at CERN and gaining valuable experience at world-renowned research facilities like CSIC, OIST, DESY, European XFEL and Stanford University. Passionate about promoting collaboration across research teams and connecting academia with industry, with a long-term vision of launching a startup tech company.
