Interactive Learning
Educational Tools

Our suite of interactive tools is designed for researchers, educators, students, and the curious public — from facility deep-dives to guided science modules.

Beam parameters through a quadrupole

Track how a quadrupole alters your beam parameters by reshaping its transverse phase space ellipse, focusing one plane while defocusing the other.

Interactive
Chromaticity through a quadrupole

Pass your beam through a quadrupole to focus it, causing high-energy particles to focus further downstream and low-energy particles closer.

Interactive
Negative, zero and positive chromaticity

Tune your beam’s chromaticity to positive for stability, zero for perfect focus, or negative to allow downstream drifting.

Interactive
Correction of achromaticity with a sextupole

Optimize sextupole strengths in high-dispersion regions to instantly eliminate momentum-dependent focal shifts and restore a uniform beam focus.

Industry Partners
Beamline Component Suppliers

Example companies that provide optics, precision positioning, and beam conditioning components frequently used around synchrotron beamlines.

XDS Oxford

They specialize in entire, end-to-end beamline engineering. This includes massive infrastructure components like insertion devices (undulators and wigglers), advanced monochromator systems for filtering specific X-ray wavelengths, and full in-vacuum, multi-purpose experimental end stations.

Visit
SmarAct

PI provides heavy-duty yet incredibly precise sample positioning stages, multi-axis goniometers, six-degree-of-freedom hexapods (used to align complex X-ray optics mirrors), and vacuum-compatible/cryogenic manipulation systems for hostile radiation environments.

Visit
RI Research Instruments GmbH

They are highly sought after for state-of-the-art X-ray mirror systems and ultra-low vibration cryocooler systems. They are also unique for producing specialized experimental setups, such as full-field and scanning X-ray microscopes (TXM/STXM) and protein crystallography end stations.
