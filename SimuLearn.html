Acknowledgement

This project was developed and presented by my team at the IUPAP WG-13 Hackathon during the 17th International Particle Accelerator Conference (IPAC 2026) in Deauville, France, where it earned first place.

SimuLearn: An interactive platform designed to bring accelerator technology into classrooms.

This project expands on existing MAX IV simulation software, and with integrated beam dynamics observables, a customisable ring builder, and a practical case study based on SESAME (Synchrotron-light for Experimental Science and Applications in the Middle East), located in Jordan. The project was proposed by MAX IV, Lund University Professors Sverker Werin and Francesca Curbis, and presented to the IUPAP WG-13 jury by:

  • Wiktoria Wiatrowska (SOLARIS, PL)
  • Helena Alamprese (Michigan State University, US)
