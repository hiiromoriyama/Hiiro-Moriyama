The parable of the two brothers

There were two brothers who crossed a desert to visit the Mahabodhi temple.

"How do you feel about this place?" asked the older brother.

"I don't know." the younger one replied. "There's nothing special."

"It's the place where the Buddha supposedly attained enlightenment."

Although widely circulated as being true, the story surrounding the famous tree was, to the younger brother, apocryphal.

"Sit beneath the sacred Bodhi Tree with your legs crossed," said the older brother. "Be aware of your inadvertently recurring condition of mind and body, that is to be in the domain of sleeping, but rather like in a meditative state. Set up noble mindfulness and clear comprehension before you."

And so the younger brother followed what he's been told.

Several hours have gone by and the younger brother was still sitting, holding his body upright.

"How do you feel now?" The older brother asked once more.

"I feel divine," said the younger brother, and he was awakened.
