This work explores local atomic environments using synchrotron radiation, focusing on the precision mapping of interstitial sites.

Probing Local Atomic Environments

EXAFS provides a unique window into the short-range order of materials. By measuring the modulation of X-ray absorption, we can determine the exact distance and species of neighboring atoms.

Interstitial Dynamics

In the simulation above, the H-Jump function illustrates how Hydrogen atoms migrate through the metallic lattice. This diffusion is a critical factor in hydrogen embrittlement research, where structural stability is mapped through precise interatomic coordination analysis.
