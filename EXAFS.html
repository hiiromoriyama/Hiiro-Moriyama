Probing Local Atomic Environments

EXAFS provides a unique window into the local structure of materials. Unlike traditional diffraction, it does not require long-range order, allowing for the precise measurement of interatomic distances and coordination numbers in complex, disordered lattices.

Interactive Lattice Dynamics

Observe the behavior of Hydrogen atoms (red) within the Fe matrix (grey).

Interstitial Hydrogen Jumps

The simulation above illustrates the stochastic nature of Hydrogen Jumps. By adjusting the controls, you can observe how thermal energy and concentration affect the frequency with which Hydrogen atoms migrate between interstitial sites—a core phenomenon in hydrogen embrittlement studies.
