This project focuses on the application of EXAFS to probe local atomic environments. Utilizing high-intensity synchrotron radiation, we resolve short-range order governing macroscopic properties.

Probing Local Atomic Environments

EXAFS is a premier spectroscopic technique used to determine the local geometric and electronic structure of matter. Unlike diffraction-based methods, EXAFS does not require long-range periodicity, making it invaluable for studying amorphous materials and liquids.

Interactive Dynamics: Fe-H Lattice Model

This visualization models the interference patterns of photoelectrons scattering within an Fe-H system. Use the H-Conc slider to introduce hydrogen impurities and observe the resulting lattice strain.

The physical principle relies on the modulation of the X-ray absorption coefficient at energies just above the absorption edge. Through advanced Fourier transform analysis, we extract precise interatomic distances and coordination numbers.
