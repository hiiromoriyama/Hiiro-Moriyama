HCP Lattice Dynamics: Zirconium Hydrides

While cubic systems provide a foundation, many engineering materials, such as Zirconium, crystallize in a Hexagonal Close-Packed (HCP) structure. notice how the lower symmetry creates a more complex path distribution.

Note (Zr-H): Purple spheres represent Zirconium; Hydrogen occupies octahedral interstitial sites.

BCC Lattice Dynamics: Hydrogen in α-Iron

In BCC metals like Ferritic Iron, Hydrogen diffusion is significantly faster. Observe the physics-based distortion: insertion of Hydrogen causes anisotropic expansion.

Note (Fe-H): Silver spheres are Iron; Red spheres are Hydrogen in tetrahedral sites.
